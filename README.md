# ChxrCky
---
## Core engine:
---
**Replay Engine:**
Record in Practice mode and reach 100%, and press playback, and the replay will play.

**TPS:**
Sets your ticks per second. ( Currently being fixed )

**Anti-Collision:**
Makes your icon go through solid blocks & spikes.

**Speed changer, And Audio:**
While playing or practicing a level, you can adjust the speed, so you can practice the level you're currently, or wanna beat playing.

**Frame Stepper:**
Makes the player stops at a specific frame, and can step frame by frame
---
## Features:
---
**Click sounds:**
Importing click packs in your clicks folder, will make your click sound whenever you press.
**UI border changer:**
Self explanatory for its name lmao
**Theme = macro format:**
20 themes, and the active theme decides the macro file extension (Cat = .meow, Vintage = .vinyl, Molten rocks = .lava, ...). Macros are saved in the mod's save folder, `macros/<Theme> theme macros/`. Old .cky macros and macros from other themes still load.
**Hitbox trail:**
Draws the player's hitboxes left behind every step. Length and colors (outer/inner) are adjustable.
**HUD:**
Shows the buttons for speed changer, & you can adjust the speed in game.




⚠️ Soft warning ⚠️ 
This utility is, and can ban you from geometry dash leaderboard stats. Use offline, or use it on a copy of a level.
