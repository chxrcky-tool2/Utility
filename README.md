# ChxrCky
---
## Core engine:
---
**Replay Engine:** Record in Practice mode and reach 100%, and press playback, and the replay will play.
**TPS:** Sets your ticks per second. ( Currently being fixed )
**Anti-Collision:** Makes your icon go through solid blocks & spikes.
**Speed changer, And Audio:** While playing or practicing a level, you can adjust the speed, so you can practice the level you're currently, or wanna beat playing.
**Frame Stepper:** Makes the player stops at a specific frame, and can step frame by frame
---
## Features:
---
**Click sounds:** Importing click packs in your clicks folder, will make your click sound whenever you press.
**UI border changer:** Self explanatory for its name lmao
**HUD:** Shows the buttons for speed changer, & you can adjust the speed in game.




## ⚠️ Soft warning ⚠️ 
This utility is, and can ban you from geometry dash leaderboard stats. Use offline, or use it on a copy of a level.

## Platform:
Android32 & Android64.
